Do / don’t & accessibility gate WCAG 2.2 AA · WAI-ARIA patternsrelease checklist

DoOne named dialog, one body scroll owner, visible required/immutable boundaries, draft-safe errors, real component instances, 44px mobile targets.
structural clarity
Don’tSplit RuntimeSelector into fields, disguise native selects, nest interactive controls, use color alone, or close the dialog when Escape dismisses a child popup.
no component theater
KeyboardTab stays trapped in the host. Escape closes the innermost layer. ArrowDown enters the listbox from search; provider radios take arrows plus Home/End; focus returns to the opening trigger.

Focus & contrast2px accent focus indicator on composite widgets; form controls pair a strong border with a ring. Essential small text reaches 4.5:1 against its rendered surface.

Unique IDs and ARIA references; a label on every control; provider rail and model list semantics kept distinct; Default plus all seven reasoning efforts exposed; favorite toggle separate from options; 360px and reduced-motion verified; default, open, empty, error, stale, no-model, and disabled states captured.
